KariYoga Paddle Yoga Bermuda

Reconnect mind and body through a beautifully curated floating paddle yoga experience set within the calm turquoise waters surrounding Mangrove Bay, King’s Point, Daniel’s Head, and the Cambridge Beaches coastline.

Led by Kari, these guided ocean wellness sessions combine mindful movement, flexibility, breathwork, balance, and relaxation while guests are anchored together on our signature floating Hatha paddle board yoga island concept surrounded by Bermuda’s crystal clear waters and peaceful coastal scenery. Each experience is thoughtfully curated to create a calming and restorative connection with the ocean in a setting far removed from a traditional studio environment.

Designed for individuals, couples, groups, wellness retreats, beginners, and experienced practitioners alike, every session can be tailored toward a softer meditative flow, mindful stretching, balance-focused movement, or a more energizing ocean wellness experience depending on the guest preference and group dynamic.

Whether experienced during the stillness of sunrise, the warmth of golden hour, or the calm transition into sunset, Kari Paddle Yoga blends luxury wellness, ocean therapy, movement, and Bermuda’s natural beauty into one unforgettable experience on the water.

Guests also have the option to elevate the experience with professional lifestyle photography and cinematic drone coverage designed to capture the beauty, atmosphere, and peaceful energy of their time on the ocean.
