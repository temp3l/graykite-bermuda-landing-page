Helpful details

What to bring and what to know.

Will I get wet?

In beginner classes, possibly, but usually not much. More advanced sessions are more likely to get splashed.

What if I am not a strong swimmer?

A lifejacket can be provided if it helps you feel safer and more comfortable.

What should I bring?

Bring enthusiasm and a smile. If you're bringing a phone or purse, use a waterproof case or leave them safely on the beach.

What should I wear?

A snug bikini or board shorts and a top work well. Everyone should use sunscreen.

Where is the meeting point?

The session starts at Somerset Long Bay.

Is there a larger lesson range?

The same lesson family includes beginner classes, beach fit, acro SUP yoga, and glow-in-the-dark night tours.
